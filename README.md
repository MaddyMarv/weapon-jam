### [Weapon Jam](https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/1207)
<img width="442" height="161" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fdb4e2d1-b201-4b58-b70b-345eb7da34db" />

A standalone mod that introduces random weapon malfunctions when firing your ranged firearms. When your weapon jams, pulling the trigger produces a dry-fire click, and you must successfully execute a Helldivers-style directional stratagem combo (`▲`, `▼`, `◄`, `►`) to clear the malfunction and restore your firearm to working order! 

Works on xbox!


**Options:**
- Enable Options (Mod, Activation Jam, Audio Effects)
- Shot Jam Chance (%)
- Activation Jam Chance (%)
- Activation Jam Severity [Special-Only Lockout, Offensive Lockout (Allow Block/Pushing), Full Weapon Lockout]
- Combo Length
- Allow WASD Inputs
- Force Jam Hotkey (Hotkey)
- Force Unjam Hotkey (Hotkey)
- HUD Position [Top Center, Below Crosshair, Bottom Center]
- HUD Scale
