Adds random weapon jams to ranged weapons, requiring a Helldivers-style arrow combo to unjam it. Works on Xbox controllers.

[b]Options:[/b]

[b]General[/b]
- Enable Mod
- Shot Jam Chance (%)
- Enable Activation Jam
- Activation Jam Chance (%)
- Activation Lockout Mode [Special-Only Lockout, Offensive Lockout (Allow Block/Pushing), Full Weapon Lockout]
- Combo Length
- Allow WASD Inputs
- Enable Audio Effects
- Force Jam (Hotkey)
- Force Unjam (Hotkey)

[b]HUD & Display[/b]
- HUD Position [Top Center, Below Crosshair, Bottom Center]
- HUD Scale
